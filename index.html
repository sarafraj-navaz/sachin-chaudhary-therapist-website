About

A calm, confidential space to be heard

I am committed to promoting mental health and emotional well-being through a safe, confidential, and non-judgmental space where you can speak openly, without fear of being judged or misunderstood.

Together we can work through stress, anxiety, work pressure, relationship and family issues, financial stress, academic challenges, career confusion, and difficulties with self-confidence — at a pace that feels right for you.

My aim is to walk alongside you as you build clarity, resilience, and the tools for lasting emotional well-being and personal growth.

Therapy isn't about fixing what's broken — it's about understanding yourself well enough to grow into who you want to be.

My Approach

Collaborative, empathetic, and evidence-informed

I work collaboratively with each person I see, building a relationship grounded in empathy and trust. Sessions focus on understanding the patterns in your thoughts, emotions, and behaviour — and how they show up in daily life.

  • Practical coping strategies tailored to your situation
  • Building emotional resilience for the long term
  • Setting realistic, achievable goals together
